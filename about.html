Who We Are

PROTECTING WHAT

Elite Shield Security Services is a licensed and insured private security company headquartered in Ruiru, Kiambu County, Kenya. We deliver professional, reliable, and technology-driven security solutions across East Africa — protecting businesses, residences, institutions, events, and VIP clients.

Our Mission

To provide professional, reliable, and technology-driven security solutions that protect people, property, and assets while maintaining the highest standards of integrity and service excellence.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and innovative security service providers in Kenya and across East Africa, setting the standard for professional security excellence.

Our Personnel

ELITE TRAINING.
ELITE PROTECTION.

All our security officers undergo rigorous professional training in surveillance, access control, emergency response, customer service, and conflict management — fully in accordance with the guidelines of the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA).

Regular specialized VIP protection training is also conducted to ensure our officers guarding high-profile clients are always mission-ready. First aid certification, firearms handling, and K-9 operations are core competencies for specialized units.
